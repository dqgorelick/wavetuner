Faceplate Divergence

The system as built is one style family — monochrome technical minimalism with a single composure dial (school, Braun ⇄ Bauhaus). This sheet explores what sits outside it: twelve style directions, a grammar for auto / arp / playhead residents, eight packers beyond shelves and columns, and the mood-board sources behind all of it. Sketches are moods, not renders — every one is drawn at the pane's 393 × 292 proportion.

Twelve Directions01

Each direction is a point; the generator should get the axes (ground · ornament · label mode · color logic · material — the map at the end of this section). Cost chips: render = drawing change only, vocab = GENES change (batch it — featurize shift, fixtures re-export, Swift re-port), packer = layout algorithm first.

Silkscreen Schematic

vocab
ARP 2600 · Serge · block-diagram panels

Printed lines draw the real signal flow (shape → fold → filter → reverb, from the manifest + engine chain); families sit in thin silkscreen boxes named in the border break. Unlike the hardware it quotes, ours is true per-song. ornament: flowlines; strongest with the signal-path packer.

Color-Block Paper

vocab
Serge paper faces · Mondrian

Family sections become filled tinted zones on paper — the wash does the grouping that hairlines do today. Deliberately violates the house “no fills except state” rule; that's why it reads as a different instrument. sectionGround: none|wash|block; reopens the paper-palette question.

Naked Circuit

vocab
Pocket Operators · Landscape Stereo Field

The plate as its own PCB: trace-and-pad under-layer, controls seated on pads, R12-style refdes micro-labels. Traces follow the same derived routing as the schematic — two skins over one graph. Gate hero: off; a giant knob on a board reads wrong.

Test Bench

vocab
HP · Tektronix · cassette futurism

The style with the strongest claim on the subject — it is an oscilloscope app. Graticule scope, engraved tick skirts, amber tabular readouts, annunciator chips. And the auto-playhead becomes a literal sweep. scopeFrame: graticule, chipStyle: annunciator.

Dot-Matrix Discipline

render
Elektron · LED step grids

A corner of the existing space made total: segments/band knobs only, jitter locked 0, seven-segment numerals, backlit matrix cells. A recipe's locks almost reach it today — the cheapest first trial of a “total look.”

Mandala

packer
Soma Lyra-8 · Patterning

Radial symmetric layout: retune (or scope) at center, voices on the inner ring, tone/filter/space outward, mirrored linework. The full break from shelf grammar — needs the radial packer, a polar ERGO pass, and tangential-or-absent labels.

Wordless Glyphs

vocab
Ciat-Lonbarde · TE pictograms

Labels replaced by generated pictograms — the wave itself for shape, a rolloff curve for filter, decaying pips for reverb. Readouts become tiny bars. Localizes for free; a11y names stay verbatim. labelMode: text|glyph|hidden.

Poster Plate

vocab
Make Noise · Bauhaus, past the current dial

school: 1 is still polite. Add a generative art layer derived from the patch (the fold curve, the wave at ghost scale), push hero to 2.2–2.5×, compose on the diagonal. The art sits under ~INK(0.08) so it stays a ground, not a second picture.

Functional Candy

vocab
TR-808 · Casio VL-1

Color logic flips from decoration to meaning: hue = family (tone orange, filter yellow, space blue, action red; voices keep the spectrum). The loudest direction and the most learnable — color teaches the plate. Must survive the CVD check (PALETTE-CVD-REPORT.md exists for a reason).

One-Bit

render
Playdate · Return of the Obra Dinn

Two colors, zero grays — state carried by ordered dither. A third entry in THEMES, not new genes: the cheapest radical direction on the board. The ink-alpha grammar (0.85 / 0.3 / 0.12) needs a dither mapping table and the visibility floor re-derived.

Ambient Field

packer
Bloom · Borderlands Granular

The panel dissolves: no dividers, no labels, controls as soft orbs gravity-clustered by family, the scope full-bleed behind everything. Probably granular-recipe-only — and it's the direction that finally gives grains their witness: a cloud, not a wave.

Material Plate

render
Braun hardware · Arturia, restrained

Rams, not photorealism: two-stop gradient caps, a highlight rim, inset fader tracks, soft ground shadow. The only direction that changes rendering without touching one layout decision — material: flat|soft|engraved can sweep independently of everything else.

UI Residents — auto · arp · playhead02

Current law: transport is chrome, the plate admits only immediate controls, auto lives in the sequencer pane (§3j), and the countdown survives on-plate as a display (cycleReadout: ring | bar). The divergence below either extends the display grammar (safe) or fuses auto into an element the plate already owns (the one defensible rule-bend).

The flywheel — auto joins retune, not the rail

The flywheelZero new elements, zero space: retune (already w 1.0, already wearing the cycle ring) gains auto as a mode of itself. Tap = retune, long-press = auto, ring track brightens when armed. The control stays immediate — arguably not a chrome-rule break at all.
Arp residentsSame four cataloged params, better drawings: a step strip in the segments grammar (doubles as the on/off indicator), a direction dial whose face is the contour. Immediacy split: arpOn + arpDir → plate; arpBpm is shared transport tempo → pane. All still blocked on ArpPatch.

A progress grammar, not a progress bar

Scope playheadA 1px sweep crossing the witness over the dwell. On the Test Bench style it is literally an oscilloscope trigger sweep — the most WaveTuner answer to “progress bar.”
Perimeter fuseA hairline consumed clockwise around the pane's border. Loud on Poster / One-Bit plates, silent everywhere else.
Divider runnerA dot traveling an existing hairline — the cycle bar dissolved into chrome the plate already has. The Braun-end answer.
Stem countdownSpectrum-bar stems dim one per subdivision; the root dims last. Progress lives where pitch already lives.
Hero drainWhen hero is on, a wedge drains inside the hero's own ring — the focal object carries the clock. The Bauhaus-end answer.
Pip trailThe chord-position pips already owed (TODO-songs P1), with the active pip filling as its dwell runs. Finite songs only — parametric keeps the ring grammar; there is no position in ∞.

One clock, many faces. Every form reads the same CYCLE_PROGRESS, and the F5 lesson stands: a paused form vanishes — it never freezes, because a frozen ring is a lie.

Eight Packers03

Beyond shelves and columns. Every candidate keeps the contracts: seeded determinism (fixed iterations, no wall-clock), re-lay-never-re-roll, the 50 pt knob cull, an ERGO pass (or its polar equivalent), one picture per plate, shrink-to-fit last. Trialed behind ?packer= — and each will need its own cap constant discovered by rating, the way 1,600 votes gave rows its caps (2 rows 68 % liked · 4+ rows ≤ 10 %).

1 · Signal-pathBlocks ordered along the real audio chain, voices as a parallel bus. The layout means something — and it's the cheapest radical packer: columns with an engine-derived section order, orderSeed gated off.
2 · Radial ringsPolar packer: center anchor, voices ring 1, tone/filter ring 2. Caps become RING_CAP + per-ring arc budgets; level/pan marriage = mirrored placement. Needs a polar ERGO.
3 · Bento treemapGuillotine split, cell area ∝ family weight-sum, each cell packs internally with existing row logic. With section washes (§01.2) this is the Serge plate. Enforce min cell aspect; re-split on slivers.
4 · Perimeter bezelControls ring the pane edge, the scope huge at center — scopePlace: hero at its conclusion. ERGO inverts: the bottom edge is prime thumb territory, so action goes there deliberately.
5 · Spectral registrationmixerScatter generalized plate-wide: every per-voice stack x-registers to its stem. The plate becomes a continuation of the spectrum. Close pitches resolve by marriage-snap — stems never move.
6 · Orbit clustersSeeded force-relax — fixed 120 iterations, mulberry32 stream, round to 0.5 pt — so it stays deterministic. Families as wells, glued pairs as stiff springs. Cull + ERGO run after, as repairs. Pairs with Ambient Field.
7 · Diagonal cascadeRows with a monotonic offset ramp, hero at the origin — composed asymmetry instead of jitter noise. It's the rows packer plus a deterministic schedule: a reason the excluded rows code stays alive.
8 · Nested containmentFamily boxes drawn as panels-in-panel, boxes packed by the column packer. Grouping becomes explicit borders — which frees gapScale to go tight without losing legibility. Exactly what dense 6-voice plates need.

If Only Three Things Get Tried05
  • 1 · The ?style= / ?packer= session params. The enabling mechanism, patterned on ?voices — banner chip while active, never leaks into exports or the app sim. Everything else is a sketch until the lab can draw it without the pipeline paying.
  • 2 · Test Bench + the scope playhead. Strongest claim on the subject — it is an oscilloscope app — mostly enum-value cost, and it answers the auto-playhead ask with the most WaveTuner answer possible: progress as a sweep on a scope.
  • 3 · Signal-path packer + flowlines. Layout that means something, implementable as a gated variant of the column packer, and it sets up the PCB skin for free.

Cost discipline: new enum values and new genes shift featurize — taste-model re-fit, fixtures re-export, Swift re-port. Batch every survivor of the rating sessions into one “vocabulary v2” round; never trickle them.
