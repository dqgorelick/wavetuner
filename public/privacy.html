just waves

privacy policy · effective 12 august 2026

just waves does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers that receive anything from the app. Everything the app does happens on your device.

Microphone

With your permission, just waves listens to your surroundings and analyzes the incoming sound in real time to drive the tuning and the visuals. That analysis happens entirely on your device, moment to moment.

The app has no recording capability. Audio from your microphone is never written to storage, never sent anywhere, and does not persist after it has been analyzed.

Camera

With your permission, just waves uses the camera to render the visual layer you see on screen. Frames are processed on your device as they arrive and are never saved, exported, or transmitted. The app cannot record video.

Local network

The listen-along feature lets one device share a session with others on the same Wi-Fi network. Devices discover each other and connect directly. What travels between them is the sound settings — synth parameters — so each device can render the sound itself.

No audio is transmitted, no personal information is included, no server sits in the middle, and nothing leaves your local network. This only operates while you host or join a session.

What stays on your device

Songs and patches you save, audio you choose to import, and your preferences are stored in the app's own storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Third parties

just waves contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no advertising. No third party receives information about you through this app.

Children

just waves does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of the app collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy information will be updated to match. The effective date above will change.
